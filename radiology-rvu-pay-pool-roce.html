Radiology RVU model

Pay for a study is cases × the RVU of that study × the rate per RVU for the radiologist’s grade. Caps stop a grade from absorbing scarce CT and MRI slots; work beyond a cap still counts as RVU but is paid at the reduced rate set on the pay tab. The pool is the share of departmental surplus handed back after every cost is met, and return on capital employed annualises each machine’s EBIT against its written-down value plus working capital.
