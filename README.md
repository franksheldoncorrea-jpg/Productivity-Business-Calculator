**Why should a decision on a ₹10–15 crore imaging asset depend on scattered Excel sheets?**

That question led me to build a personal **Radiology Business Calculator**.

Radiology decisions often require connecting doctor payouts, equipment-level P&L, volumes, and ROCE across multiple calculations.

So I built a browser-based tool that brings these together in one place:

* RVU-based doctor payout
* Equipment-wise P&L
* Modality-level ROCE
* Scenario-based financial analysis

The objective wasn't to replace financial models—it was to create a single view of **productivity, profitability, and capital efficiency** to support more structured business decisions.

A personal project at the intersection of **Healthcare × Finance × Technology**.
